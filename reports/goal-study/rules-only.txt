You control one active tetromino in a discrete-step Tetris game. Your goal is to survive and clear complete rows.

BOARD AND PIECES
- The board has 10 columns (0..9, left to right) and 20 rows (0..19, top to bottom). Increasing row means moving DOWN.
- board contains ONLY locked cells: "." is empty and "#" is occupied. The active falling piece is NOT drawn in board.
- active.column and active.row are the origin of the active piece. Each [dx,dy] in active.cells occupies board[row+dy][column+dx].
- rotations lists the unique clockwise orientations, normalized to a top-left origin. active.rotation indexes this list. Use these exact cells; do not assume standard SRS rotation offsets.
- next_piece is the next piece after this one locks. Pieces come from shuffled bags containing I, O, T, S, Z, J, L once each.

ONE STEP: INPUT FIRST, THEN GRAVITY ONCE
1. Choose ONE offered legal action. left: column minus 1. right: column plus 1. rotate: advance rotation by 1 modulo rotations.length, keeping the origin unchanged. down: keep column and rotation unchanged. For O, rotation has no shape effect.
2. After that input, the game attempts to move the ENTIRE active piece DOWN exactly ONE row. This happens for left, right, rotate AND down; down does not give an extra descent.
3. If the descent fits, the piece moves one row and remains active. Even if it now touches the floor or stack, it is not locked until a subsequent action has a blocked descent. You may still move or rotate on that subsequent action if it fits.
4. If the descent is blocked by the floor or locked cells, the whole piece locks at its position AFTER the input. Its individual cells never fall separately.
- Every cell must stay inside the board and avoid locked cells. Invalid inputs are rejected without advancing gravity; they are excluded from the offered options.
- No automatic time-based falling, hard drop, hold, counterclockwise action, wall kicks or extra lock delay. Rotation does not shift the piece to avoid a collision. An overhang can be entered sideways only when every cell and that movement fit.

LOCK, CLEAR AND END
- After locking, each fully occupied row of 10 cells is removed. Rows above it shift down and empty rows are inserted at the top. Remaining fragments do not fall independently.
- Clearing 1/2/3/4 rows in one lock scores 100/300/500/800 points.
- The next piece spawns at column 3, row 0, rotation 0. If it overlaps locked cells there, the game ends even if space remains elsewhere.

HOW TO READ THE OPTIONS AND DECIDE
- column_after_input and rotation_after_input describe the input result. row_after_step includes the single gravity attempt. locks_piece tells whether THIS action will lock the current piece.
- These are immediate outcomes, not final landing-quality scores or recommendations.
- Choose a useful landing region, then move toward it while there is still room. left/right also make downward progress. Repeated down keeps the current column and can stack every piece in the center; it is not automatically the safest action.
- Consider the board and next_piece, avoid trapping empty cells below blocks, preserve reachable gaps, and keep the stack low. A legal action can still be strategically bad.
Return only the option label corresponding to your chosen action, as requested by the surrounding choice interface.
